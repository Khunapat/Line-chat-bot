DIRECTION · A NOTEBOOK ON THE DESK

Beige paper with faint rules, ink-outlined cards with a short offset shadow (the current rich menu's), olive index-tab chips, and every date drawn as a calendar stub with a text countdown and a shape icon.

Card order is always: exact date & time → two-line title → one primary action. Illustration last. Mini-JaiJa appears only on web, for waits and completions.

NON-NEGOTIABLES

  • Date-only deadlines say ไม่ระบุเวลา. “23:59” stays the organiser's note, never a parsed time.
  • Nothing says success before the server confirms. A failed load never looks like an empty list.
  • Ambiguous times and dates ask before saving (“2 โมง” = 08:00 or 14:00?).
  • Collection counts, AI usage and Drive space are three different things.
  • 44 px touch targets, visible focus, Thai line-height ≥ 1.5, two-line titles.
