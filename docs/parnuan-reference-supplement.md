## Motion and limitations

Feature selection changed highlights, text section and phone demonstration; anchor navigation changed URL to #how and positioned the section. Decorative illustrations and phone presentation were visible. No duration, easing, parallax, automatic-advance interval or animation frame rate was measured; all timing remains unverified. Observed changes between screenshots are not measurements. Homepage demonstration appeared in different feature states over the session, but auto-advance versus user/scroll-driven behavior was not isolated.

Parnuan authenticated screens, actual receipt parsing, budgets, export, group permissions and notification delivery were not inspectable beyond LINE Login without continuing authentication. Education, careers, contact and terms pages were linked but not inspected; public product/pricing/FAQ content was read. No error was intentionally induced and no financial information was entered. Public marketing features must be labeled Advertised, not Tested, in downstream designs.

Sources: [Public product site](https://parnuan.com/), [feature walkthrough](https://parnuan.com/#how), [app entry](https://app.parnuan.com/liff). These were read in the browser; no external pricing or vendor cost assumptions are used.
